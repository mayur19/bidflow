# Risk Register

Likelihood and impact: Low / Medium / High. Reviewed at each milestone exit.

| ID   | Risk                                                                               | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation                                                                                                               | Owner role        | Status                           |
| ---- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------- | ------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------- | -------------------------------- |
| R-01 | Cross-tenant data leak through a query missing `organization_id`                   | Medium     | High   | Repository convention (ADR-0003), 404 for foreign ids, integration tests per module, tenant sweep BF-057, RLS evaluation | Tech lead         | Mitigating (departments covered) |
| R-02 | Sealed bid contents visible to buyers or competitors before close                  | Medium     | High   | Supplier namespace and DTOs (ADR-0015), comparison returns 409 before close, B-05/B-06 tests (BF-033, BF-055)            | Tech lead         | Open                             |
| R-03 | Rounding or float errors make PO totals disagree with the awarded bid              | Low        | High   | Integer minor units, single half-up rule, 100 % domain coverage (ADR-0013, BF-007)                                       | Tech lead         | Mitigated                        |
| R-04 | Unapproved or over-budget purchase becomes a PO                                    | Medium     | High   | Approval steps (ADR-0016), transactional budget check with row lock (ADR-0018), audit of denials (BF-043)                | Product owner     | Open                             |
| R-05 | Duplicate award approval or receipt creates double financial records               | Medium     | High   | Unique constraints, conditional updates, Idempotency-Key (BF-041, BF-045, BF-047), B-17 test                             | Tech lead         | Open                             |
| R-06 | Late bids accepted due to client clock or race at the deadline                     | Medium     | Medium | Server clock comparison inside the transaction (ADR-0017), B-08 test (BF-031, BF-035)                                    | Tech lead         | Open                             |
| R-07 | Scoring perceived as automatic award decision                                      | Medium     | Medium | UI labels score as decision support; award requires recommendation, justification and approval (ADR-0014)                | Product owner     | Open                             |

| R-09 | Estimates too optimistic for UI-heavy tickets (wizard, matrix, portal)             | Medium     | Medium | 6 h cap per ticket, re-estimate on pickup, cut visual polish before integrity (PRD §10)                                  | Delivery lead     | Monitoring                       |
| R-10 | Uploaded files used for stored XSS or malware delivery                             | Medium     | Medium | Allow-list, magic-byte sniffing, attachment disposition, tenant-checked downloads (ADR-0011, BF-022)                     | Security champion | Open                             |
